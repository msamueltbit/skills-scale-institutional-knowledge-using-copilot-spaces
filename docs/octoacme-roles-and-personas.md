# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Additional Roles

These roles may be combined or omitted when an initiative does not need a dedicated person for each function. Assign a clear owner for each relevant responsibility and decision, even when one person covers multiple roles.

## Engineering / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering or Technical Leads guide technical direction and coordinate engineering work so the solution is maintainable, feasible, and aligned with project goals.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture and technical decisions
- Coordinate implementation, engineering estimates, and technical dependencies
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Support code quality and consistency across Developers

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers on implementation choices, reviews, and technical problem-solving
- Align with the Product Manager on feasibility and product trade-offs
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on estimates, dependencies, and delivery risks

---

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA or Test Leads coordinate quality activities and provide evidence that the product meets acceptance criteria and is ready for its intended use.

### Responsibilities
- Define the test approach and coverage for the initiative
- Coordinate validation against acceptance criteria, including integration and end-to-end testing where appropriate
- Track defects, communicate quality risks, and report readiness
- Identify opportunities to improve testability and prevent regressions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers to plan testability, investigate defects, and verify fixes
- Work with the Product Manager to clarify acceptance criteria and expected behavior
- Keep the Project Manager informed about quality risks, dependencies, and readiness

---

## UX / Product Designers and Researchers

### Role Summary
UX or Product Designers and Researchers ensure that solutions reflect user needs and provide clear, accessible, and usable experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs and share relevant findings
- Design and iterate on user flows, interfaces, and prototypes
- Assess usability and accessibility concerns
- Help validate design decisions with users and product outcomes

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with the Product Manager to connect user needs, product goals, and priorities
- Work with Developers to communicate designs and resolve implementation questions
- Coordinate with QA / Test Leads to include usability and accessibility considerations in validation
- Keep the Project Manager aware of design dependencies and review needs

---

## Release / Operations Leads (DevOps or SRE)

### Role Summary
Release or Operations Leads prepare and support reliable deployments and ensure the service can be monitored and operated after release.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate deployment readiness and operational requirements
- Prepare monitoring, alerting, and release verification plans
- Identify operational risks and document rollback or mitigation steps
- Support incident response and operational handoffs where applicable

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with the Engineering / Technical Lead and Developers on infrastructure, deployment, and reliability needs
- Work with QA / Test Leads on smoke tests and release verification
- Align with the Project Manager on release timing, readiness, and operational risks

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security or Privacy Leads advise on security and privacy requirements and help teams identify and manage related risks.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security and privacy requirements early
- Review relevant designs, data flows, and risks
- Recommend safeguards and appropriate security or privacy validation
- Communicate unresolved concerns and escalation needs

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to clarify requirements and trade-offs
- Coordinate with Developers and the Engineering / Technical Lead to incorporate safeguards into the solution
- Work with QA / Test Leads to validate relevant controls
- Inform the Project Manager of risks, dependencies, and decisions requiring escalation

---

## Customer Support / Service Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support or Service Representatives bring customer-facing and operational support perspectives into planning and delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Share recurring customer feedback and support trends
- Identify support, training, and documentation needs
- Prepare support teams for upcoming changes and releases
- Route post-release issues and emerging customer impact to the delivery team

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Share customer insights with the Product Manager to inform priorities and success measures
- Coordinate readiness, communications, and issue escalation with the Project Manager
- Work with QA / Test Leads and Developers to clarify user-facing issues and confirm fixes

---

## Executive Sponsors / Business Decision Makers

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors or Business Decision Makers provide business direction, confirm significant decisions, and help resolve constraints beyond the delivery team's authority.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm the business outcome and strategic alignment
- Make or authorize major priority, scope, or resource decisions
- Resolve escalated organizational constraints
- Review progress and significant risks at agreed milestones

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Align with the Product Manager on outcomes and major product priorities
- Receive status, risk, and decision requests from the Project Manager
- Consult with delivery leads when decisions depend on technical or operational considerations
